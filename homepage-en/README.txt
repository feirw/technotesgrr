Technotes homepage (English, standalone)

Copy this whole folder anywhere.

How to open
- Double-click index.html, or
- If videos do not play from a file:// URL, serve the folder:
    npx --yes serve .
    then open the URL it prints.

Needs internet once for Tailwind CDN and the Manrope font.
Everything else (images, videos, icons) is inside assets/.
